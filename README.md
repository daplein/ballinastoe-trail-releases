# Ballinastoe Trail 🚵

**A hands-free mountain bike companion for Ballinastoe Woods, Co. Wicklow.**

Phone in your pocket: it knows which trail you're on, tells you the way at junctions ("Start of Pig Dog. Left: Pig Dog. Straight: Red loop."), times your runs automatically and races you against your last or best run. The screen matches the weather, from Met Éireann's forecast.

> ⚠️ **First version.** It works on the trails' map and in practice rides; expect rough edges under the trees. Ride within your limits and keep your eyes on the trail.

## Install (about a minute)

1. Open the [install page](https://daplein.github.io/apps/ballinastoe-trail/) **on your Android phone** and tap **Download for Android**.
2. When Chrome asks **File might be harmful**, tap **Download anyway** (it asks that for every app that isn't from the Play Store).
3. Tap **Open**. When Android asks, allow Chrome to **install unknown apps** (one time only), then tap **Install**.
4. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap **More details → Install anyway**. That's expected: the app isn't from the Play Store.
5. At the trailhead, open the app and tap **Start ride**. Allow location while using the app.

When a new version is out, an **Update** button shows on the home screen. New versions install over the old one; your rides and best times are kept.

## What it does

- **Live map** of the Ballinastoe trails (Red, Blue and Black loops and the named singletrack) with your position and the trail you're on.
- **Spoken directions at junctions**, relative to the way you're facing. Music ducks while it speaks.
- **Automatic timing** of every one-way section (Pig Dog, Zipper, Holts, Junkers…), with checkpoints so short-cuts don't count.
- **Ghost racing** against your last or best run: "5 seconds ahead of your best", "New personal best!"
- **Weather and daylight:** a spoken forecast at the start, heavy-rain alerts, and sunset warnings with the time back to the car park.
- **History** of rides and best times, each ride's route on the map, GPX export.
- **Practice rides** to try it all at home.
- Optional: **voice commands** ("Hey Ballinastoe, where am I?") and a **"Ready to ride?"** prompt when you reach the car park. Both off by default.

## Privacy

- No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no server.
- Your rides stay **on your phone**. Location is only used during a ride you start (and, if you switch on the arrival prompt, to notice you've reached the car park).
- Voice commands, if you switch them on, are recognised on the phone and only during a ride.
- The app fetches map tiles from OpenStreetMap, the forecast from Met Éireann, and asks [daplein.github.io](https://daplein.github.io/apps/ballinastoe-trail/latest.json) once per launch whether a newer version is out (nothing is sent).
